Hardware

Parts you can source anywhere

A LiDAR, a single-board computer, a powerbank, a microSD card, a few screws and four printed parts. Only a screwdriver with M3 and M2.5 bits is needed.

PartNotes
Livox Mid-360Main sensor. Mid-360S also supported
NanoPi Zero 2Wi-Fi module required, no eMMC needed
microSD card64 GB minimum, U3/V30 or faster
PowerbankTwo outputs: 10 W+ and 12/20 V PD. Tested: HPS99
LiDAR cableRigid 0.5 m or flexible 0.2 m
USB-C PD trigger12 or 20 V, soldered to the power end
V-mount plateOptional, for a panorama camera on top
M3×8 ×4LiDAR to body
M2.5×12 ×4NanoPi to body
Fixator 1/4"Handle or tripod mount
Printed partshead, guard, stand, cover · PLA or PETG · 0.2 mm · supports on body only

See the scan while you walk

The phone connects to the scanner over its own Wi-Fi. While you scan, the app draws the plan-view cloud and your trajectory, shows four quality metrics and the recording time left.

Field workflow

From power-on to point cloud

  1. 01

    Power on

    Connect the powerbank. Recording starts automatically.

  2. 02

    Hold still for 5 s

    Keep the scanner flat and still, then lift it slowly.

  3. 03

    Walk the space

    Follow the live map and the scanning tips in the app.

  4. 04

    Stop

    From the app, or just disconnect the power.

  5. 05

    Copy the scan

    To a USB drive on the scanner, or straight to your phone.

  6. 06

    Convert and process

    Batch-convert the recordings on your PC and build the point cloud.

FAQ

Questions before you build

Is OpenSLAM free?

The hardware design and STL files are published under the MIT license. Remote software setup is a paid option.

How does remote setup work?

You insert a microSD card into your PC and join a short remote session. We provide the license file and run the SD Card Installer. Then you put the card in the scanner and it is ready.

Can I pay with advertising?

Yes, if you have an audience in scanning, surveying, construction tech or robotics. Message us on Telegram and tell us about your channel.

How accurate is it?

We compared OpenSLAM clouds with total station measurements on a real site. The comparison video is on our YouTube channel.

Can I get colored point clouds?

Yes. Mount an Insta360 camera on the optional V-mount plate. The pipeline colors the cloud from the 360° footage and builds a panoramic tour.

Do you work with teams outside Russia?

Yes. Setup and support are remote, so your location does not matter.
